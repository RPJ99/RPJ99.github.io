Things to try

  • Change the channel height and watch the maximum flow. Predict the change before you move the slider.
  • At 3,000 µL/min, switch to uniform flow. The outlet reads about 4 °C hotter: that is the error from ignoring the velocity profile.
  • Push the flow well past the maximum and watch cold fluid escape down the centre of the channel.
  • Drop to 100 µL/min and zoom to 0–0.5 mm. The fluid reaches wall temperature within a fraction of a millimetre.

Model and assumptions

Same physics as coupled_flow_heat.py: the Graetz problem, u(z)·∂T/∂x = α·∂²T/∂z², marched along the channel with a Crank–Nicolson scheme. Walls held at fixed temperature; heat enters through top and bottom (side walls neglected, about a 15 % effect); fully developed velocity profile from the inlet; water properties at about 60 °C held constant; conduction along the channel neglected. The solver reproduces the textbook fully developed Nusselt numbers, 7.541 (parabolic) and 9.870 (plug).
